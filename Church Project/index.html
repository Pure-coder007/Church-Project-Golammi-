Welcome to Gateway of Life and Miracle Ministries International

Golammi Worship Center

GOLAMMI is a prophetic ministry where God reconstructs anything the devil has destroyed in the life of people. We are a people of faith, love, and power, dedicated to winning souls and building disciples for Christ.

Prophet (Dr.) James Elinissi Iferemilekun (Lion Of Jesus)

Founder & General Overseer

Our Mission

To reconstruct broken lives, restore destinies, and bring people into their God-given purpose through the prophetic word, prayer, and the power of the Holy Spirit.

Our Vision

To be a global gateway of life and miracles, raising a generation of prophetic believers who will impact their world with the love and power of Jesus Christ.

Our Values

Faith in God's Word, Love for all people, Prayer and Intercession, Prophetic Worship, Community Impact, and Excellence in all we do.
