Our Story

Gateway of Life & Miracle Ministries Int'l

Gateway of Life & Miracle Ministries Int'l (Golammi Worship Centre), also called Mountain of Reconstruction and the City of Prophecy, was founded on November 17, 2002.

The church started from a room at 29, Sariyu Olusesi Street, Agodo Egbe, Cele Egbe Bus Stop, Ikotun-Isolo Road, Lagos. The ministry began with 3 people. The second Sunday service recorded 7 people, and the third Sunday service recorded 12 people in attendance. As attendance kept increasing, the church later moved into the parlour.

By the second year, because of the rapid growth of the church, the ministry leased half a plot of land at 17, Olushola Street, Agodo for two years. Before the lease elapsed, God had spoken through the prophet that the church would not spend the whole two years there before moving to the permanent site. God was faithful to His word, and the permanent church land was secured before the end of the lease period.

On July 7, 2007, the church held her first service on the permanent site at 17, Adeyinka Street, Agodo Egbe, in the same locality where the ministry started. That site remains the headquarters of the church, from where people from many nations are being blessed.

Prophet Dr. James Elinissi Iferemilekun

Founder & General Overseer

Known as the Lion of Jesus, Prophet Elinissi is the founder of Gateway of Life & Miracle Ministries Int'l and carries a prophetic mandate to reconstruct mankind, awaken destinies, and restore divine order through the power of the Holy Spirit.

Founder’s Prophetic Journey

Key parts of the life story and calling of Prophet Dr. James Elinissi Iferemilekun.

Prophetic Birth

His birth was preceded by prophetic declarations across generations, including the word that a son would arise to end the hold of family idolatry and become a Prophet of God.

Signs At Birth

At the time of his delivery, a prophet redirected his mother into a church where he was born, and water sprang up from the ground in a miraculous sign marking God’s hand upon his life.

Divine Preservation

From infancy, God preserved him from evil attacks. His prophetic calling manifested from childhood, and by age six he had already begun to prophesy.

Biography Highlights

Selected details from the founder’s life, training, and ministerial calling.

Education & Craft

He attended St John Anglican School, Idemo, St Paul Odo Ado, and Mary Hills Boys School, Oke Ila, and also mastered carpentry and furniture work with excellence.

Prophetic Training

Under Prophet David Falade, he was enrolled in the school of prophets and was ordained as the seer of the realm at age 12 and the leader of the realm at age 18.

Creative Calling

He was deeply involved in theatre, cultural performance, and Yoruba traditional poetry, leading a theater group of about 40 crew members across Southwestern Nigeria.

Years Away

At age 20 he left Ado-Ekiti, passed through difficult years in Lagos and other parts of Nigeria, and lived in the secular world for ten years before God called him back.

Revelation At 30

On his 30th birthday in Port Harcourt, he received a revelation in which Prophet Moses Orimolade handed him a sword and rod and declared it was time to start his ministry.

Ministry Release

Before starting his own ministry, he served under Baba Aladura Ogunkunle and Superintendent Apostle Joseph Tunolase, who later blessed and released him into his assignment.
