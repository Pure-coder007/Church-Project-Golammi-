Our Story

Gateway of Life and Miracle Ministries International

Gateway of Life and Miracle Ministries International, popularly known as Golammi Worship Center (GWC), is a prophetic ministry birthed by divine mandate to bring hope, healing, and restoration to a broken world. The ministry was founded by Prophet (Dr.) James Elinissi Iferemilekun, affectionately called the "Lion of Jesus," a man of God with a burning passion for souls and a deep prophetic mantle.

The journey of GOLAMMI began over 25 years ago when God spoke to Prophet James in a vision, commissioning him to go forth and "reconstruct everything the devil has destroyed in the life of people." What started as a small prayer gathering in a humble home has now grown into a global ministry with thousands of members, multiple outreach programs, and countless testimonies of miracles, healing, deliverance, and transformation.

The name "GOLAMMI" is an acronym that represents the heart of the ministry: Gateway of Opportunity, Life, and Abundance through Miracle Ministries International. It is a prophetic declaration that every person who walks through the doors of GOLAMMI will experience a divine shift—a reconstruction of their destiny, health, finances, and relationships.

Over the years, GOLAMMI has become a beacon of light and hope, known for its powerful prophetic worship, life-changing prayer sessions, and unwavering commitment to the Word of God. The ministry has established itself as a place where the lost find their way, the broken find healing, and the hopeless find a reason to believe again.

Prophet (Dr.) James Elinissi Iferemilekun

Founder & General Overseer — The Lion of Jesus

Prophet James is a dynamic speaker, prolific teacher of the Word, and a prophetic voice to this generation. He is married to his beautiful wife, Pastor (Mrs.) Esther Iferemilekun, and they are blessed with wonderful children. His passion for souls and his unwavering faith in God's power to restore has touched thousands of lives across the globe.

The Journey of Faith

What began as a humble prayer group in the living room of Prophet James and his wife has blossomed into a vibrant, multi-cultural congregation that spans across nations. The ministry has witnessed:

  • Thousands of souls won for Christ through evangelistic crusades and outreach programs
  • Countless testimonies of healing from terminal diseases, including cancer, HIV/AIDS, and heart conditions
  • Deliverance from demonic oppression, addiction, and generational curses
  • Financial breakthroughs and career advancements through prophetic prayers
  • Restored marriages, reconciled families, and transformed relationships
  • The raising of next-generation leaders through the youth and children's ministries

Our History Timeline

2001

The Birth of GOLAMMI

Prophet James receives the divine mandate to establish a prophetic ministry focused on reconstructing broken lives. The first prayer meeting is held in their home with just 5 people.

2003

First Crusade & Growth

The first open-air crusade is held with over 200 people attending. Many give their lives to Christ, and the first miracle of healing is recorded.

2005

Establishment of GOLAMMI Worship Center

The ministry acquires its first permanent worship center, officially named Golammi Worship Center. Membership grows to over 500.

2010

International Reach

GOLAMMI expands internationally with branches established in multiple countries. The ministry's radio broadcast begins, reaching thousands of listeners.

2015

Prophetic Conference Launch

The inaugural Annual Prophetic Conference is launched, attracting thousands of attendees from around the world for a week of prayer, worship, and prophetic impartation.

2020

Digital Expansion

GOLAMMI embraces digital platforms, launching live streaming services, online prayer sessions, and a robust social media presence to reach a global audience.

2024

2024 & Beyond

GOLAMMI continues to grow, expanding its outreach programs, establishing new branches, and impacting lives across the continents with the message of hope and reconstruction.

What We Believe

Our foundational beliefs are rooted in the Word of God and the power of the Holy Spirit.

THE BIBLE

We believe in the divine inspiration and authority of the Holy Scriptures. The Bible is the Word of God, infallible, and the final authority for faith and practice.

THE TRINITY

We believe in one God, eternally existing in three persons: God the Father, God the Son (Jesus Christ), and God the Holy Spirit. They are co-equal and co-eternal.

SALVATION

We believe that salvation is a free gift from God, received through faith in Jesus Christ. We are saved by grace through faith, and not by our own works.

BAPTISM

We believe in water baptism by immersion as a public declaration of faith in Jesus Christ. We also believe in the baptism of the Holy Spirit as a distinct experience after conversion.

HEALING & MIRACLES

We believe in divine healing and the operation of the gifts of the Holy Spirit. Miracles, signs, and wonders are the inheritance of every believer and are active in the church today.

THE SECOND COMING

We believe in the imminent return of Jesus Christ. We look forward to the resurrection of the dead and the final judgment, where God will establish His eternal kingdom.
