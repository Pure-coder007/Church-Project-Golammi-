Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Gateway of Life and Miracle Ministries International.

Home / FAQ

What is Gateway of Life and Miracle Ministries International?

Gateway of Life and Miracle Ministries International, also known as Golammi Worship Center (GWC), is a prophetic ministry founded by Prophet (Dr.) James Elinissi Iferemilekun. We are a people of faith, love, and power, dedicated to winning souls and building disciples for Christ through prophetic worship, prayer, and the Word of God.

What are the service times?

Our service times are: Sunday: 9:00 AM & 11:00 AM (Prophetic Worship Services), Wednesday: 6:30 PM (Bible Study & Prayer), Friday: 6:00 PM (Prayer & Deliverance Service).

Where is Golammi Worship Center located?

Golammi Worship Center is located at 17, Adeyinka Street, Agodo, Cele Egbe Bus Stop, Ikotun Isolo Road, Lagos. You can also join us online through our live streaming services.

Do you offer children's church?

Yes, we have a vibrant Children's Church during our Sunday services. Our children's ministry provides age-appropriate teachings, worship, and activities to help children grow in their faith and knowledge of God.

What should I expect at a service?

You can expect a powerful time of prophetic worship, heartfelt prayer, and a life-changing message from the Word of God. Our services are filled with the presence of God, and you will experience a warm, welcoming atmosphere.

How can I give my tithes and offerings?

You can give online through our secure giving platform on the Giving page. You can also give in person during our services. We accept tithes, offerings, prophetic seeds, and building fund contributions.

Is online giving secure?

Yes, our online giving platform uses industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your information. Your payment is safe and secure.

How can I submit a prayer request?

You can submit a prayer request through our Prayer page. Our intercessory prayer team will pray for you and your needs. You can also call our 24/7 prayer line at 09032804020 or 08060998511.

What is the prayer line?

Our prayer line is a 24/7 telephone service where you can call and speak with a prayer partner. You can receive prayer, encouragement, and support at any time. The numbers are 09032804020 and 08060998511.

How can I join the membership?

To become a member of Gateway of Life and Miracle Ministries International, you can attend our New Members Class, which is held quarterly. Please contact our office or speak with a pastor after service for more information.

How can I get involved in ministry?

There are many ways to get involved! You can join one of our ministries such as the Worship Team, Prayer Ministry, Youth Ministry, Outreach, Children's Church, or Media Team. Speak with a ministry leader after service or fill out a volunteer form.

Still have questions? Contact us and we'll be happy to help.
