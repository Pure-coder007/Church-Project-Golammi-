Golammi Worship Center

GOLAMMI is a prophetic ministry where God reconstructs anything the devil has destroyed in the life of people. We are a people of faith, love, and power, dedicated to winning souls and building disciples for Christ.

Our Mission

To reconstruct broken lives, restore destinies, and bring people into their God-given purpose through the prophetic word, prayer, and the power of the Holy Spirit.

Our Vision

To be a global gateway of life and miracles, raising a generation of prophetic believers who will impact their world with the love and power of Jesus Christ.

Our Values

Faith in God's Word, Love for all people, Prayer and Intercession, Prophetic Worship, Community Impact, and Excellence in all we do.

A Prayer for Salvation

Do You Want to Receive Jesus Today?

If you're ready, pray this prayer with all your heart:

Heavenly Father,

I come to You just as I am. I acknowledge that I am a sinner and I need Your forgiveness. I believe that Jesus Christ died on the cross for my sins and rose again. I confess with my mouth that Jesus is Lord. I invite You, Lord Jesus, to come into my heart and be my Lord and Savior.

I repent of all my sins and turn away from my old ways. I ask You to forgive me, cleanse me, and make me new. I am ready to follow You for the rest of my life.

Thank You for loving me and saving me. I am a child of God. In Jesus' name, Amen.

If you prayed this prayer, you are now a child of God!
