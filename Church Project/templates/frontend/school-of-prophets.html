Gateway School of Prophets

The Gateway School of Prophets is a divine mandate established to identify, train, and release prophetic voices into the body of Christ. We are committed to raising a generation of prophets who will speak with accuracy, operate in the gifts of the Spirit, and demonstrate the love and power of God.

Whether you are already operating in the prophetic gift, sense a calling, or simply desire to grow in prophetic ministry, this school is designed to equip you for effective service in God's vineyard.
